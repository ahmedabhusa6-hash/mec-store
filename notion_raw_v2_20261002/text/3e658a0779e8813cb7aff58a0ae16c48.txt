Insight — Marketing is a multi-actor value system, not a promotion function
========================================
Evidence basis
AMA definition frames marketing around creating, communicating, delivering and exchanging offerings of value across multiple stakeholders.
Pearson's 19th-edition structure covers marketplace analysis, marketing information, buyer behavior, value-driven strategy and mix, channels, communications, competitive/global context, sustainability and ethics.
Interpretation boundary
This is a conceptual synthesis for the knowledge spine. It is not a tested causal model and does not rank marketing channels.
