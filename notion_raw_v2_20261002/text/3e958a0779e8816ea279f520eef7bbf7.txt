Deep Research / Fresh Evidence
FACT — Cloudflare Durable Objects
Cloudflare Durable Objects are available on Free and Paid plans. SQLite-backed Durable Objects are available on Free; current Free limits include 5 GB total SQLite storage, 100 Durable Object classes, 100,000 requests/day, 100,000 rows read/day, and 100,000 rows written/day. Durable Objects provide stateful coordination primitives suitable for distributed systems.
Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/
Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/limits/
FACT — Cloudflare Queues
Cloudflare Queues are available on Free and Paid plans and provide guaranteed delivery, retries, delays, batching, and Dead Letter Queues. Free currently includes 10,000 operations/day and up to 10,000 queues; Free message retention is 24 hours. Paid retention defaults to 4 days and can be configured up to 14 days.
Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/queues/
Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/queues/platform/pricing/
FACT — Cloudflare Workflows
Workflows remains available on Free and Paid and provides durable multi-step execution, retries, persisted state, and observability. Free has explicit execution/step/state/retention limits.
Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/workflows/reference/limits/
FACT — Render
Render Free Key Value is in-memory only and loses state on restart. Free Postgres expires after 30 days and has no backups.
Source: https://render.com/docs/key-value
Source: https://render.com/docs/free
FACT — GitHub Repository Creation
GitHub's REST API supports POST /user/repos; fine-grained tokens require Repository creation write or Administration write permission. The connected GitHub tool surface still does not expose an authorized repository-creation action.
Source: https://docs.github.com/en/rest/repos/repos
