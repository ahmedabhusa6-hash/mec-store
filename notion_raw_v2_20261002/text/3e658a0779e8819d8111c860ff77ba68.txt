Case — QuickBooks ProAdvisor + Product Trial
========================================
Evidence summary
QuickBooks documents a 30-day free trial for QuickBooks Online, allowing prospective customers to test the product before subscription.[1]
Intuit's current ProAdvisor documentation describes the program as serving accounting, bookkeeping, tax, and consulting professionals, with training, resources, marketing tools, and other benefits. The August 2026 update also describes changes intended to strengthen the relationship with accountants and lists marketing-related tools and client-engagement resources for qualifying tiers.[2]
Analytical interpretation
Insight: QuickBooks separates two acquisition mechanisms that can reinforce each other: direct product evaluation and trusted professional influence.
Evidence boundary
The public evidence verifies the mechanisms. It does not, by itself, establish that one path produces better unit economics or conversion than the other.
Candidate reusable pattern
Dual-path Acquisition: Product Trial + Professional Influence — pending cross-company synthesis.
