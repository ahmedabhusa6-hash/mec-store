UPD-003 — ONYX distributor program operational design verified
========================================
Verified operating design
The public ONYX distributor page documents a multi-step channel process rather than a simple contact/referral mechanism.
Evidence boundary
This establishes the designed operating process. It does not establish that every listed distributor follows the process, nor the commercial results produced by it.
Historical-count boundary
The same page contains a statement of more than 88 branches/points/agents explicitly tied to the end of 2017. That figure is historical and is not used as a current count.
