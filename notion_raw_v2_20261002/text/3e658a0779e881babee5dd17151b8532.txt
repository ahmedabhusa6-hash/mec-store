Benchmark — ONYX / YemenSoft vs Global Knowledge Patterns
========================================
Benchmark Matrix
Important boundary
The previous benchmark statement that a formal partner-marketing capability was not established is superseded by the current public evidence reviewed on September 25, 2026.
The current evidence establishes partner capability in the Ultimate Solutions / ONYX ecosystem. It does not by itself establish that YemenSoft directly owns or operates the program, nor does it establish commercial effectiveness.
“Not established in reviewed public evidence” remains an evidence gap, not “does not exist”, for any capability not yet documented.
Current ONYX-specific knowledge
ONYX ERP is publicly presented as a broad multi-solution portfolio.
Current YemenSoft news documents education and institutional relationship activity.
The portfolio spans accounting, sales, distribution, HR, production, POS and digital-finance-related solutions.
Required next layer
Verify the organizational/legal relationship between YemenSoft and Ultimate Solutions.
Map the partner program to actual ONYX/YemenSoft channel operations.
Obtain internal evidence for partner activation, lead routing, pipeline and revenue attribution.
Only then assess transferability of the global Partner Marketing pattern to ONYX.
