Deep Dive — Xero Marketing System
========================================
1 Demand
Problem-oriented guides, templates, calculators and business resources create discovery opportunities before product selection.
2 Trust
Customer stories plus accountant/bookkeeper relationships add proof and professional influence.
3 Conversion
Free-trial path reduces evaluation friction.
4 Channel multiplication
Partner toolkit gives third parties campaign assets, SEO/social guidance, email templates, webinars and campaigns.
5 Measurement gap
Public evidence confirms capabilities; it does not provide comparable CAC or channel revenue attribution.
Reusable architecture: utility/content + professional trust + low-friction evaluation + partner amplification.
