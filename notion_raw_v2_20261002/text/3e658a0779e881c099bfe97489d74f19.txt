Case — Xero Partner Marketing Enablement
========================================
Evidence summary
Xero's official partner toolkit includes client-facing guides and brochures, SEO guidance, social media resources, a step-by-step campaign-in-a-box, email templates, event/webinar resources, brand assets, and industry marketing playbooks.[1]
Xero also documents a partner marketing fund in the US, where approved partner marketing activities can be reimbursed after review and supporting documentation.[2]
Analytical interpretation
Insight: Xero is not only supplying a referral relationship; it is supplying the partner with operational marketing infrastructure.
Evidence boundary
This case establishes the existence and structure of the capability. It does not prove that partner marketing is Xero's most effective acquisition channel or quantify its incremental revenue impact.
Candidate reusable pattern
