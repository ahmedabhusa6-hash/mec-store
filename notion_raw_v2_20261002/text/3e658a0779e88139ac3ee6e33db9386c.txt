Case — Odoo Partner Ecosystem + Lead Routing
========================================
Evidence summary
Odoo's latest public partnership agreement describes official partner levels and lists visibility on Odoo.com, sales coaching and webinars, access to marketing material, partner-event support/promotion, commissions, and dedicated account-management capabilities for qualifying levels.[1]
Odoo's CRM documentation describes reseller lead routing, including forwarding leads to partners and automatic assignment based on designated partner level and location.[2]
Analytical interpretation
Insight: Odoo operationalizes the partner channel inside the sales system rather than treating partnership as an external relationship only.
Evidence boundary
This demonstrates system capability and commercial structure. It does not establish causal marketing effectiveness or the ROI of the partner channel.
Candidate reusable pattern
Operationalized Partner Distribution — partner enablement + incentives + lead routing; pending cross-company synthesis.
