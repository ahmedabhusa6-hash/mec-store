Deep Dive — NetSuite Partner Demand System
========================================
Demand creation
Co-branded materials and sample campaigns lower partner campaign design costs.
Funding
Cooperative marketing funds can subsidize approved demand-generation activities.
Attribution
Campaign management connects leads/closing/co-marketing ROI at partner level.
Governance
Funding and campaign controls introduce an operational approval layer.
System insight: partner enablement becomes more valuable when activity can be attributed, not merely executed.
