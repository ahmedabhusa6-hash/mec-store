Deep Dive — Odoo Marketing System
========================================
Acquisition
Partner visibility + Odoo brand + ecosystem discovery.
Activation
Sales coaching, webinars and marketing material reduce partner activation friction.
Conversion
Leads can be forwarded/assigned to resellers; partner level/location affects routing.
Fulfillment
Implementation partner handles local delivery, strengthening distribution capacity.
Ecosystem
Apps/integrations and partners increase solution coverage.
System insight: distribution is embedded in operational CRM rather than left as a purely external referral process.
