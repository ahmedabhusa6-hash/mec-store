Why Do You Need to Complete Identity Verification Before Selling on GamsGo?

Account Verification — Ensuring Safe and Reliable Dealing


ccount verification ensures safer and more reliable transactions.
Thousands of users have already verified their GamsGo accounts, freely and safely buying and selling various digital goods and services on the platform.
To ensure every user can enjoy a fair and trustworthy trading environment, the system may require you to provide identification documents or credit card information for identity verification.


✅ Valid Documents for Verification


Any government-issued valid document can be used for verification, including but not limited to: ID card, passport, driver’s license, student ID.
Make sure the following information is clearly visible in the photo:
Name, surname, date of birth, expiration date, and your photo.
Blurred, covered, or edited images will not pass verification.


💬 Why is identity verification required?


GamsGo is committed to providing every user with a safe, transparent, and fraud-free trading environment.
The purpose of the verification process is not only to prevent fake accounts and scams, but also to:

  • Ensure sellers are authentic and reliable, protecting buyers' rights;
  • Prevent risks such as money laundering, account theft, and fraudulent transactions;
  • Optimize the platform’s credit system, giving high-credit sellers more exposure and resource allocation.

Once verified, you will immediately receive the platform’s trust badge, making buyers more willing to trade with you and improving your sales success rate and brand image.


💼 Platform Protection Mechanism

For account-based products, the platform sets a reasonable funds hold period based on the product type to ensure smooth transaction completion.
If malicious fraud is discovered after the hold period, GamsGo reserves the right to take legal action.


🔐 Data Security and Privacy Protection


At GamsGo, user privacy and data security are our top priorities.
We use multi-layer encryption technology to protect all information and strictly adhere to international privacy protection standards such as GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

  • All verification materials are used only for identity confirmation and will never be used for other commercial purposes.
  • Data will be automatically and securely deleted within 30 days after verification completion.
  • The platform uses bank-grade security systems to prevent unauthorized access and data breaches.


Your trust is the driving force behind GamsGo’s continuous improvement of service quality.
