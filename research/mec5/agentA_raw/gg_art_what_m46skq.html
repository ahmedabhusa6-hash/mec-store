What Fees Does GamsGo Charge During Product Sales?

To provide merchants with a stable, secure, and efficient trading environment, and to continuously optimize the platform experience, GamsGo charges a commission based on the type of product. The commission is deducted from successful orders and is calculated only after the transaction is completed.
Through reasonable fee settings, we can continuously invest resources in:

  • ✅ Risk control
  • ✅ Payment and settlement system maintenance
  • ✅ Marketing and user growth initiatives
  • ✅ Continuous upgrades and improvements to online systems and security features to meet higher standards


The establishment and maintenance of these systems help make transactions fairer, safer, and smoother.


1. Commission Rates for Different Product Categories


Currently, the platform sets commission rates for different categories as follows:

Different types of virtual products have varying levels of transaction risk, encryption and verification difficulty, and user protection costs. Therefore, the platform adjusts rates reasonably based on operational and risk management costs, ensuring stable development while continuously supporting merchant growth.


2. Does the Platform Charge Any Additional Fees?


Currently, the platform does not charge any listing fees, deposits, or withdrawal fees. Merchants only need to pay attention to the order commission. If new services are added or rates adjusted in the future, the platform will provide clear notifications.
