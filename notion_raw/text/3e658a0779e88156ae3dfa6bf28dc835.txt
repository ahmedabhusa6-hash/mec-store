# Functional Scope — Accounting Systems Taxonomy Node
Claim: Functional Scope must be a first-class dimension in the accounting-systems taxonomy.
Definition: classify a product by the breadth and integration of its business/financial functions, not by the vendor's category label.
Observed evidence: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance documents GL, AP, AR, budgeting, cash/bank, cost accounting, fixed assets and financial reporting; SAP S/4HANA Cloud General Ledger integrates with asset accounting, AP/AR, management accounting, procurement and treasury; Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials includes GL, intercompany, payables, receivables, payments, cash management, tax, expenses and assets; Xero explicitly contrasts accounting software's financial focus with broader ERP scope while noting the boundary can blur.
Applicability: taxonomy design and product classification.
Limitations: all evidence in this first cycle is vendor-published; an independent accounting/ERP taxonomy source is still required before treating the boundary rule as fully validated.
