# Vesterinen et al. (2024) novelty statement vs Jung & Boo (2022) unified SEM model
Verification
Vesterinen et al. presents a conceptual framework linking BDAC, marketing agility and firm performance and explicitly states a literature gap around a unified model. cite https://doi.org/10.1080/10696679.2024.2322600
Jung & Boo (2022) explicitly reports an empirical SEM study of BDAC, exploration/exploitation, market agility and firm performance using survey data from 250 companies. cite https://doi.org/10.11627/jksie.2022.45.3.150
Decision
Do not use Vesterinen et al.'s broad novelty sentence as evidence that no unified model previously existed. Preserve the narrower, defensible interpretation: the paper proposed a conceptual framework with a claimed gap that may refer to its particular formulation, mechanism detail, or conceptual scope.
No KNG-43 promotion or semantic change is justified by this reconciliation alone.
