# CKC-RSH17-007 — Full mediation evidence calibration
Result
An independent South Korea SEM study adds observational support for a mediated BDAC → organizational exploration/exploitation → market agility → firm performance model. This strengthens structural replication of the mechanism but does not establish causal mediation.
Decision
RSH-17 remains Needs Follow-up and KNG-43 remains SYNTHESIZED. The valid next research unit is a causal/longitudinal mediator test.
