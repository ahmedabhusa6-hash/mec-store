# Architecture — Accounting Systems Taxonomy Node
Initial rule
Architecture is a distinct taxonomy dimension and may require multiple descriptors rather than one mutually exclusive label.
Evidence-backed descriptors in this cycle
- Multi-tier / three-tier structure
- Modular / addon-based composition
- Integration and configurability as architectural characteristics
- Openness and cross-enterprise capability as relevant ERP characteristics in the independent framework
Non-assumptions
Architecture does not determine System_Type by itself. Deployment mode is also separate from architecture.
Status
ACTIVE as a taxonomy node; controlled vocabulary remains open.
