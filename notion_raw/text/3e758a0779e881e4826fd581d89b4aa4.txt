# CKC-RSH17-006 — Downstream market-agility performance evidence calibration
Evidence calibration result
Recent independent academic evidence shows that marketing agility → firm performance is not a stable universal effect. A peer-reviewed UK study differentiates short- and long-term profitability outcomes; a 2026 emerging-market configuration study finds performance associations through agility components; and a 2026 East Java SME study reports no significant direct marketing-agility effect on performance. These studies differ materially in context and design, so they support contextualization rather than a single causal conclusion.
