# EVD-RSH17-LONG-01 — Marketing agility temporally predicts competitive advantage in a two-wave Saudi retail panel, but financial-performance causality remains unestablished
Evidence_ID: EVD-RSH17-LONG-01
Research_ID: RSH-17
Source_ID: SRC-RSH17-005
Evidence type: Quantitative longitudinal evidence.
Design: Two-wave panel; T=677 and T+1=569; cross-lagged panel model.
Supported inference: marketing agility has temporally predictive influence within a data-driven innovation capability → marketing agility → competitive advantage model.
Not supported: direct causal effect of marketing agility on firm financial performance; complete analytics → market agility → financial performance mediation.
Boundary: Saudi Arabian retail context; outcome is competitive advantage.
