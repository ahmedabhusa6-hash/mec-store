# CHG-RSH11-001 — Buyer behavior and exchange foundations
Commit
RSH-11 now contains a verified foundation layer for exchange and buyer behavior.
Boundary
The buyer-behavior insight uses bibliographic/table-of-contents evidence for Howard & Sheth and a separate empirical test for model-fit limitations. It does not claim universal validity.
Open gaps
Comparative buyer-model review; broader exchange-theory variants; contemporary behavioral models; measurement and cross-market boundaries.
