# SRC-RSH17-005 — Competitive advantage longitudinal marketing agility evidence
Source_ID: SRC-RSH17-005
Research_ID: RSH-17
Citation: Alghamdi, O. A., & Agag, G. (2024). Competitive advantage: A longitudinal analysis of the roles of data-driven innovation capabilities, marketing agility, and market turbulence. Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, 76, 103547.
DOI: 10.1016/j.jretconser.2023.103547
Method: Two-wave panel; T=677, T+1=569; cross-lagged panel modeling.
Bounded relevance: longitudinal temporal evidence for marketing agility and competitive advantage; not a direct financial-performance causal identification.
