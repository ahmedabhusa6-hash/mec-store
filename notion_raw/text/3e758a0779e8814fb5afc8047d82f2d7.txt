# ERP classifications can use license/implementation fees alongside organization and functionality; licensing is therefore a separate descriptive dimension, not a sufficient proxy for System Type.
Evidence role
Independent academic evidence for ASR-7.
Finding
Licensing/fees appear as one classification factor among several, while functionality is another. This supports keeping Licensing separate from Functional Scope.
Limitation
The source does not establish a universal rule that licensing cannot affect every classification; it only shows that licensing and functionality are distinct classification factors and warns about contradictory ERP groupings.
